Disaster Guidelines

Before: Identify safe spots, keep emergency kit ready, participate in drills.

During: Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Stay away from windows, heavy objects, and glass.

After: Evacuate calmly to assembly point, check for injuries, help others if safe.

Before: Keep emergency kit ready, know higher ground and evacuation routes.

During: Move to higher ground, avoid walking/driving through floodwater, follow alerts.

After: Return only when safe, report hazards, assist in recovery if possible.

Before: Know fire exits, extinguishers, and emergency assembly points.

During: Stop, Drop, and Roll if clothes catch fire, evacuate calmly using stairs.

After: Report incidents, check for injuries, avoid re-entering unsafe areas.

Before: Secure windows and doors, have emergency kit ready, stay informed.

During: Stay indoors away from windows, follow evacuation instructions if needed.

After: Check surroundings, avoid downed wires and flooded areas, follow official instructions.

Before: Maintain hygiene, know isolation areas, keep masks and sanitizers ready.

During: Follow distancing rules, wear masks, report symptoms to authorities.

After: Continue hygiene practices, follow recovery protocols, stay informed.
