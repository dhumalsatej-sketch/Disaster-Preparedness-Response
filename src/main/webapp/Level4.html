Emergency Contact Numbers

Police

Call for any crime, threat, or urgent help.

Call 100

🚒Fire Brigade

Report fire accidents or smoke emergencies.

Call 101

🚑Ambulance

Medical emergencies, injuries, or health danger.

Call 102

Disaster Management (NDMA)

Natural disasters, floods, earthquakes, storms.

Call 108

👶Child Helpline

Help for children in distress or danger.

Call 1098

Women Helpline

For women's safety, harassment, or danger.

Call 1091

Quick Safety Tips

  • Stay calm and call the appropriate emergency number immediately.
  • Share clear, quick information about your location and the problem.
  • Follow authorities’ instructions during disasters.
  • Do not panic or mislead emergency responders.
  • Always keep emergency contacts saved in your phone.
